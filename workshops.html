How I work

I learn most by practising language in real settings and facilitating workshops in unfamiliar contexts. I rely on non-formal education and learning by doing, and I sometimes use movement and circus as a tool.

Background

Juggler and street artist. I joined Saltimbanco Italiano in Dubai as a 19-year-old juggler, and after a few years, thanks to English, I also helped manage parts of it. Translator and founder of Learntrain. English C2, French B2, Japanese B1, currently learning Chinese.
